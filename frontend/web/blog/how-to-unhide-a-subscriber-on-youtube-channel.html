How to Unhide a Subscriber on YouTube (Step-by-Step)

By The VidSEOKit Editorial Team· September 18, 2026· 5 min read· 1,110 words

Learn how to unhide a subscriber or user on your YouTube channel via YouTube Studio, plus key updates on comment moderation and subscriber count rules.

Understanding What 'Unhiding' Means on YouTube Today

Managing a growing YouTube community requires precise control over comment sections, live chats, and moderation tools. However, there is frequent confusion around the term "unhiding a subscriber" due to past updates to the platform. Creators typically mean one of two distinct actions when searching for this topic:

  1. Unhiding a user (subscriber) from your channel: Removing a previously restricted or shadowbanned commenter from your Hidden Users list so their comments become visible to the public again.
  2. Unhiding your channel's total subscriber count: Displaying your channel's public subscriber tally on your homepage (a feature YouTube altered significantly in recent years).

In this tactical guide, we will break down both scenarios step-by-step, explaining how to manage your moderation queue in YouTube Studio, restore comment privileges for legitimate subscribers, and navigate YouTube's ongoing platform policies.

How to Unhide a User on YouTube Studio (Desktop)

If you previously hid a user—either intentionally during moderation or accidentally—their comments are invisible to other viewers. Unhiding them restores their ability to participate publicly on your channel. Here is the exact, step-by-step process using YouTube Studio on your desktop browser:

Step 1: Access YouTube Studio

Navigate to studio.youtube.com and sign in with the Google account associated with your YouTube channel.

Step 2: Open Channel Settings

Look at the left-hand navigation sidebar. Scroll down to the bottom and click on the Settings icon (represented by a gear symbol).

Step 3: Navigate to the Community Tab

In the pop-up settings menu, click on Community in the left column. By default, you will be placed on the Automated Filters tab.

Step 4: Locate the 'Hidden Users' Section

Scroll down until you see the field labeled Hidden Users. This box contains a list of channel avatars and usernames representing every account currently hidden from your channel.

Step 5: Remove the User and Save

Find the specific user or subscriber you wish to unhide. Click the small 'X' icon next to their username to remove them from the list. Finally, click Save at the bottom right corner of the window. The user is now officially unhidden.

Note: Unhiding a user will not retroactively publish comments they left while they were hidden. It will only apply to new comments and live chat contributions submitted after you save the changes.

How to Unhide a User via the YouTube Studio Mobile App

Managing moderation on the go is essential for active channel creators. You can also unhide users using the YouTube Studio mobile app on iOS or Android:

  1. Open the YouTube Studio app on your mobile device.
  2. Tap your profile icon in the top-right corner and select Settings.
  3. Tap Community, then select Automated Filters.
  4. Scroll to the Hidden Users list.
  5. Tap the 'X' next to the handle of the user you want to restore.
  6. Tap Save to confirm your updates.

Can You Unhide Subscriber Counts on YouTube Channel Pages?

A common question among creators is whether they can toggle their total channel subscriber count from hidden to visible. Historically, YouTube allowed creators to hide their subscriber numbers in channel settings under Advanced Channel Settings.

The July 2022 YouTube Policy Change

On July 29, 2022, YouTube officially deprecated the feature that allowed creators to hide their subscriber counts. This decision was implemented globally to combat impersonation, spam channels, and bad actors who hid subscriber counts to trick viewers into believing they were established creators or official brand channels.

Because of this update:

  • Subscriber counts are now permanently public for all YouTube channels.
  • You no longer need (nor have the option) to manually "unhide" your overall subscriber count in settings.
  • If your subscriber count does not appear, it is likely due to platform latency, an active brand account sync issue, or account suspension status rather than a hidden setting.

What Happens After You Unhide a Subscriber?

Understanding the exact technical impact of unhiding a user helps you maintain healthy community engagement:

  • Visibility Restored: Any future comment left by the unhidden subscriber will instantly undergo your default channel moderation rules (e.g., standard publishing or held for review if it contains restricted words).
  • Live Chat Access: The user can once again participate in your YouTube Premieres and live stream chats without their messages being silently filtered out.
  • No Direct Notifications: YouTube does not notify the user when they are hidden or unhidden. The user experience updates seamlessly without automated alerts.

Best Practices for YouTube Channel Moderation

Rather than relying solely on the permanent hiding of users, modern YouTube creators use layered moderation tools to filter toxic comments while keeping genuine subscribers engaged:

1. Use Blocked Words and Links

In YouTube Studio > Settings > Community, add specific keywords, phrases, or promotional URLs to your Blocked Words list. Comments containing these terms will automatically be held for review rather than publishing publicly or requiring you to shadowban the user.

2. Adjust Moderation Strictness

Under Community > Defaults, select Hold potentially inappropriate comments for review and check the box to Increase Strictness. This utilizes YouTube’s automated AI to capture borderline spam without completely disabling user accounts.

3. Assign Channel Moderators

If your live streams or video comment sections receive high traffic, assign trusted subscribers or team members as Standard Moderators or Managing Moderators. They can hold comments for review or temporarily mute problematic users without requiring full channel admin access.

Summary Checklist

To successfully unhide a subscriber and keep your YouTube community running smoothly:

  • Go to YouTube Studio > Settings > Community > Automated Filters.
  • Remove the user from the Hidden Users field and click Save.
  • Remember that channel subscriber counts are now universally public by default following YouTube's 2022 security updates.
  • Leverage keyword blocking and strictness settings to moderate effectively without losing active community members.

Frequently asked questions

Will a subscriber know if I unhide them on YouTube?

No, YouTube does not send a notification when you unhide a user or restore their ability to comment on your channel.

Can I still hide my total subscriber count on YouTube?

No. As of July 29, 2022, YouTube permanently removed the option to hide subscriber counts to combat channel impersonation and spam.

What is the difference between hiding a user and blocking them on YouTube?

On YouTube, 'hiding a user from your channel' prevents their comments and live chat messages from being visible to anyone else, effectively shadowbanning them. Unhiding them restores public visibility to their future comments.

Where do I find the list of hidden users in YouTube Studio?

Go to YouTube Studio, click Settings in the left sidebar, navigate to the Community tab, and look under the Automated Filters section to view your Hidden Users list.

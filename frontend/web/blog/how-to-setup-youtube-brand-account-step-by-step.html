How to Set Up a YouTube Brand Account (Step-by-Step)

By The VidSEOKit Editorial Team· September 18, 2026· 5 min read· 1,156 words

Learn how to set up a YouTube Brand Account step-by-step. Manage permissions, protect personal data, and scale your channel team effectively.

Advertisement

Why You Need a YouTube Brand Account

When launching or scaling a presence on YouTube, choosing the correct account architecture is a critical foundation. A standard Personal Account ties your YouTube channel directly to your personal Google identity. This means your channel name defaults to your Google Account name, and your personal email address remains inextricably linked to your public identity.

By contrast, a YouTube Brand Account operates as a distinct business entity within the Google ecosystem. It grants creators and companies several major advantages:

  • Multi-User Management: Grant team members, video editors, and managers access to your channel without ever sharing your primary account credentials or password.
  • Brand Isolation: Use a dedicated channel name, logo, and aesthetic completely independent of your personal Google identity.
  • Scalability & Transferability: Easily transfer channel ownership to a new Google account or sell the asset entirely if your business changes hands.
  • Centralized Hub: Manage multiple channels under a single master Google login.

Pro Tip: Even if you are currently a solo content creator, creating a Brand Account from day one prevents painful migration steps later when you decide to hire a thumbnail artist or video editor.

Personal vs. Brand Account Comparison

Before proceeding with the setup, review the key structural differences between personal and brand setups:

  • Personal Account: Single owner, bound to personal Google profile name, zero role delegation, higher security risk due to shared login credentials.
  • Brand Account: Multiple owners/managers, fully custom name and handle, granular permission levels (Editor, Manager, Viewer), protected personal login credentials.

Step 1: Sign In to Google

To create a YouTube Brand Account, you first need a primary Google Account. This account will act as the master owner of the brand channel.

  1. Navigate to YouTube.com in your web browser.
  2. Click Sign In in the upper right-hand corner.
  3. Log in using the Google Account you wish to use as the administrative foundation for your brand channel.

Step 2: Access the YouTube Channel Switcher

Instead of creating a basic channel through the standard user dropdown menu, you will want to access the official channel switcher tool.

  1. Once signed in, open a new tab and go directly to youtube.com/channel_switcher.
  2. This dashboard displays all current channels associated with your Google account, alongside an option to create a new channel.

Step 3: Create Your Brand Channel

Now you are ready to instantiate the actual Brand Account.

  1. On the Channel Switcher page, click the button labeled Create a channel.
  2. You will be prompted to enter a channel name. Input your business, store, or brand name here. (Note: You can update this name later without altering your primary Google profile).
  3. Check the acknowledgment box confirming that you are creating a new Google Account with its own settings and YouTube history.
  4. Click Create.

YouTube may require SMS verification depending on your account location and security profile. Follow the on-screen prompts to complete phone verification if requested.

Step 4: Moving an Existing Personal Channel to a Brand Account (Optional)

If you already built a personal channel with existing uploads, subscribers, and analytics, you can transfer that channel to your new Brand Account without losing content.

  1. Sign in to YouTube under the personal channel you wish to move.
  2. Click your profile image in the top right and select Settings.
  3. From the left navigation bar, select Advanced settings.
  4. Click Move channel to a Brand Account.
  5. Re-authenticate your identity if prompted by Google.
  6. Select your newly created Brand Account from the list of available target accounts.
  7. Review the details carefully: YouTube will confirm what transfers over (subscribers, videos, views, channel structure) and what might be removed (custom URLs or user comments).
  8. Click Move channel to complete the process.

Step 5: Assigning Team Permissions and Roles

Once your Brand Account is active, you can safely delegate access to agency partners, virtual assistants, or video editors.

Using YouTube Studio Permissions

YouTube Studio now features a integrated permissions system that works natively across web and mobile access:

  1. Log into YouTube Studio using your Brand Account profile.
  2. In the left sidebar menu, click Settings.
  3. Select the Permissions tab.
  4. Click Invite in the upper right corner.
  5. Enter the email address of the team member you wish to invite.
  6. Assign their specific access level:
    • Manager: Can add and remove users, edit all content, and view channel performance data.
    • Editor: Can edit videos, titles, metadata, and upload content, but cannot add users or delete the account.
    • Editor (Limited): Same privileges as Editor, but cannot view monetization revenue data.
    • Subtitles Editor: Can add, edit, or remove subtitles and captions.
    • Viewer: Read-only access to channel analytics and configuration.
  7. Click Save. Google will send an email invitation to the recipient, which they must accept to gain access.

Step 6: Essential Post-Setup Channel Branding

A Brand Account is only as professional as its Visual Identity and SEO setup. Complete these essential customization steps inside YouTube Studio > Customization:

1. Profile Picture & Banner Dimensions

  • Profile Picture: Upload a high-resolution logo recommended at 800 x 800 pixels (PNG or non-animated GIF, max 4MB).
  • Channel Banner: Upload a responsive banner graphic scaled at 2560 x 1440 pixels with a minimum text safe area of 1235 x 338 pixels.

2. Basic Info & Handle Selection

  • Handle (@username): Choose a short, clean channel handle that matches your brand's social media handles (e.g., @YourBrandName).
  • Description: Write a keyword-rich channel description introducing your business values, content output schedule, and core topics to help YouTube search algorithms classify your brand.
  • Contact Info & External Links: Add customer service or lead-generation links along with a primary business contact email address.

Maintaining Brand Security

To ensure long-term channel security, follow these administrative best practices:

  • Enforce Two-Step Verification (2FA) on all Google Accounts holding Owner or Manager permissions.
  • Audit your Permissions menu quarterly to remove former employees, freelancers, or inactive vendors.
  • Never share primary Google account password credentials; assign individual permissions instead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the main difference between a Personal channel and a Brand Account?

A personal account is directly tied to your individual Google profile name and email address, allowing only one owner. A Brand Account allows customized channel branding separate from your name, enables multiple managers to access the channel with their own logins, and protects personal privacy.

Can I convert an existing personal YouTube channel to a Brand Account?

Yes. You can move your existing personal channel to a newly created Brand Account via YouTube's Advanced Settings. Your uploaded videos, subscriber base, and channel analytics will automatically transfer over.

Will moving my channel to a Brand Account delete my custom URL or handle?

Moving your channel may require you to re-claim your channel handle (@username) or custom URL, but your subscribers, watch time, and monetization status will remain intact.

How many managers can I assign to my YouTube Brand Account?

You can add multiple team members with distinct roles (such as Primary Owner, Owner, Manager, Editor, or Viewer) to accommodate agencies, editors, and administrative staff.

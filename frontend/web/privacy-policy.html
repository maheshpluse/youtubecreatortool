Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CreatorTools.io LLC ("CreatorTools", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you visit creatortools.biz or use any of our free creator tools.

1. Information We Collect

The tools are anonymous and require no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, we do not connect to your YouTube account, and we do not save the titles, descriptions, tags or URLs you paste into our tools.

  • Information you provide: The text you enter into our tools (e.g., SEO Analyzer, Title Generator) is sent to our servers for processing and returned to you. We do not store this data.
  • Information collected automatically: We collect standard web log data (IP address, browser type, pages viewed) to keep the service running and secure.
  • Cookies: We use cookies for theme preferences, anti-abuse (Google reCAPTCHA), and advertising (Google AdSense).

2. Google AdSense and Advertising

We use Google AdSense to serve ads and fund our free tools. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. AI Processing

Some of our tools are powered by Google's generative AI models. When you submit a request, the text is transmitted to Google's API for processing. We do not write your inputs to a database. Google processes those inputs under its own terms.
